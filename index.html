    Good to know

    How long does it take?

    A simple website takes about 3 days. A business system like Shop Manager or Payroll usually takes 1–3 weeks, depending on how much it needs to do.

    Do I need a computer?

    No. Every system works on a smartphone. A computer is helpful for printing reports, but not required.

    Can you change it later?

    Yes. Small changes are included during your support period, and bigger additions are priced separately before we start.

    Can I have my own web address?

    Yes. We can connect a name like yourbusiness.co.ke. The name has a small yearly fee, which we'll tell you before buying it.

    How do I pay?

    Half before we start and half when you approve the finished system. Ask us on WhatsApp for payment details.
